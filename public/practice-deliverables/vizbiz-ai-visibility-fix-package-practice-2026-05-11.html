1. Executive summary

VizBiz.ai already has strong foundations for AI visibility: clear positioning, strong homepage metadata, explicit AI crawler permissions in robots.txt, an llms.txt file, a live sitemap, and multiple educational pages around AI visibility.

The biggest issue is positioning drift. The homepage now sells to local businesses broadly, while the sitemap, blog, llms.txt, about page, and many landing pages still heavily position VizBiz as a tool for car dealerships. This creates a mixed entity signal for Google and AI systems.

For AI visibility, that matters because answer engines need a simple, repeated, consistent explanation of:

  • who VizBiz serves
  • what VizBiz does
  • which categories it specializes in
  • what proof exists
  • which page is the canonical answer for each topic

Right now, the site tells two stories:

  1. Current intended story: AI visibility intelligence for local businesses.
  2. Legacy story: AI visibility audits/tools for car dealerships.

The highest-impact fix is to make the site entity consistent: either fully commit to local businesses broadly, or clearly frame dealerships as one vertical example/case-study cluster rather than the main business category.

11. Client-facing summary

VizBiz.ai has a strong AI visibility foundation, but the site still carries legacy dealership-first language across important machine-readable and human-readable surfaces. The fastest path to improvement is to align every major signal around the current offer: AI visibility intelligence for local businesses.

The first implementation sprint should update llms.txt, metadata, sitemap hierarchy, the About page, the Blog page, and the benchmark report. Then VizBiz should add a canonical local-business explainer page and methodology page so both customers and AI systems have a clean source to cite.
