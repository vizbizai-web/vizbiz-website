# VizBiz Paid Fulfillment Templates

Purpose: make the first paid clients fulfillable manually without waiting for full automation.

Offers:
- $88 One-Time Full Report + Fix
- $188/mo Monthly AI Visibility Growth Plan

Operating principle: paid deliverables must feel concrete, useful, and ethical. They should not be just “a better PDF.” They should show evidence, explain what it means, and give the business a realistic fix path.

### $88 deliverable sections

#### 1. Executive summary

```txt

Market:
Primary service/niche:
AVI score:
Overall status: strong / mixed / at risk / needs review

Biggest trust issue:
```

#### 2. AI recommendation evidence

Include:
- sampled human-like buyer questions
- appearance/miss outcome
- whether competitor appeared
- answer excerpt/source note where available
- confidence level

Keep wording platform-neutral unless provider-specific evidence is real.

Format:

```txt
Question:
Why a buyer would ask this:
Outcome:
Business appeared? yes/no/partial
Competitor appeared? yes/no/partial
Evidence excerpt/source:
What this suggests:
```

#### 3. Competitor gap breakdown

For each supplied competitor:

```txt
Competitor:
Website:
Why they may be easier for AI/search to understand:
Signals they appear to have:
Signals the client is missing or weaker on:
Risk level: low / medium / high
Fix priority:
```

Important:
- Do not show auto-discovered competitors as client-facing unless approved.
- If competitor evidence is weak, label it as needs confirmation.

#### 4. Website and machine-readiness review

Check:
- homepage clarity
- service/category page clarity
- title/meta quality
- headings
- internal links
- schema/entity clarity
- sitemap
- robots.txt
- llms.txt
- contact/location proof
- FAQ/answer readiness
- review/testimonial visibility
- trust proof on conversion paths

Format:

```txt
Signal:
Status: good / weak / missing / needs review
Evidence:
Why it matters:
Fix priority:
```

#### 5. Review and trust signal review

Check:
- Google profile clarity
- review quantity/quality
- review language matching buyer concerns
- credentials/awards/guarantees
- local proof
- before/after or case proof
- phone/address consistency

Format:

```txt
Trust signal:
Current state:
Opportunity:
Recommended improvement:
```

#### 6. 30-day fix plan

Do not overwhelm. Give first 5–8 fixes.

```txt
Fix 1:
Impact:
Difficulty:
Owner:
Exact action:
Evidence/source:

Fix 2:
...
```

Priority order:
1. fix incorrect/unclear business identity
2. clarify primary services and city/service area
3. strengthen core service pages
4. add FAQ/answer-ready content
5. improve schema/entity data
6. improve review/trust proof placement
7. create llms.txt/sitemap/robots readiness where relevant
8. add competitor-specific differentiation

#### 7. Copy/schema/FAQ fix pack

Give usable assets, not theory.

Include up to:
- revised homepage/service intro block
- 3–5 FAQ questions/answers
- schema recommendation summary
- GBP/review prompt suggestions
- llms.txt recommendation summary

Do not include unsupported code if not verified for their site/CMS.

#### 8. Closing recommendation

```txt
Best first move:
Why this should come first:
Expected benefit:
What to measure after 30 days:
Recommended next step: one-time implementation / monthly monitoring / hold
```

## Operator QA checklist before sending any paid deliverable

Check every paid report for:
- no Perplexity-only framing unless evidence is specifically from Perplexity
- human-like buyer questions
- no keyword-string fake prompts
- no unsupported competitor claims
- no unsupported revenue guarantees
- no wrong niche language
- no stale dealership copy unless dealership report
- clear distinction between evidence, interpretation, and recommendation
- every fix is actionable
- client can understand the report without knowing SEO/GEO jargon
