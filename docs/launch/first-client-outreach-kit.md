# VizBiz First-Client Outreach Kit

Purpose: get the first friendly/local businesses into the free AVI mini-report funnel without sounding like an AI grifter wearing a rented blazer.

Launch posture: founder-led, helpful, controlled, manual review.

## Target profile for first 20 prospects

Prioritize businesses where:
- owner/operator can be reached directly
- website exists but is imperfect
- reviews/local reputation matter
- local intent is obvious
- AI/search visibility could plausibly affect leads
- Alex has some community, friend, family, service, or geographic connection

Good first categories:
- dentists
- med spas / beauty clinics
- roofers / contractors
- lawyers
- restaurants / bakeries / coffee shops
- auto dealers/service centers
- gyms / wellness studios
- real estate teams
- specialty retail
- local home services

Avoid at first:
- enterprise chains
- businesses with no website
- hyper-sensitive medical/legal claims unless copy is careful
- hostile/unknown prospects where report mistakes would create reputational risk

## Positioning rule

Do not open with “GEO/AEO/AI SEO.”

Open with:
- “People are starting to ask AI tools who to choose nearby.”
- “I’m testing a quick visibility snapshot for local businesses.”
- “I can show whether AI systems can understand and recommend your business.”
- “No pressure — I’m looking for feedback from a few real businesses before wider launch.”

```txt
1. best website URL

3. two competitors customers compare you against, if you know them
```

## Objection handlers

### “I don’t care about AI.”

```txt
Totally fair. Most business owners do not care about AI as a topic.

The practical issue is simpler: customers are starting to use AI-powered search as a shortcut for local recommendations. If those tools cannot understand your business, they may recommend a clearer competitor instead.

VizBiz is not about chasing AI hype. It is about making your business easier to find, trust, and choose.
```

### “Is this SEO?”

```txt
Related, but not the same.

SEO helps search engines rank pages. VizBiz looks at whether AI-powered recommendation systems can understand your business, verify trust signals, compare competitors, and explain why someone should choose you.

Good SEO helps. But AI visibility also depends on entity clarity, reviews, structured proof, service pages, citations, and answer-ready content.
```

### “Can you guarantee rankings?”

```txt
No — and anyone who guarantees AI rankings is selling astrology with a dashboard.

What we can do is identify the gaps that make your business harder to understand or recommend: weak service clarity, missing schema, unclear location signals, thin trust proof, poor review language, or stronger competitor evidence.

We guarantee the quality of the diagnosis and fix plan, not impossible-to-control platform behavior.
```

### “Why do you need competitors?”

```txt
Because visibility is relative. A score alone does not tell you much.

If customers compare you against two specific nearby businesses, those are the names we should benchmark. That makes the report more accurate than using generic competitor guesses.
```
